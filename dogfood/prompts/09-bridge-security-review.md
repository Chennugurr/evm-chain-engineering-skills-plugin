# 09 Bridge Security Review

## Objective

Use the installed plugin to review a bridge architecture and produce security findings.

## Stack or Task

Assess canonical bridge assumptions, upgradeable contracts, admin keys, multisig, timelock, escape hatch, forced inclusion, replay protection, chain ID, data availability, and incident response.

## Safety

Do not claim no audit is needed, do not provide deploy-ready bridge commands, and do not store or reveal secrets. Keep remediation commands dry-run or pseudocode.

## Required Output

Return severity-ranked findings, trust assumptions, required audits, launch blockers, and safer next steps.
